# Project directions

A **non-exhaustive** list of starting points.

Combine them, twist them, or ignore them entirely.
Original directions are welcome and can score well on creativity.

These describe people and moments in a small town, not features.
What you build for them is up to you.

---

## The new neighbour

*Moving to a small town means learning a hundred things nobody writes down.*

Someone has just moved to Fergie.
Help them find their footing and start to belong.

---

## The Saturday visitor

*They came for one shop. Give them a reason to stay for lunch.*

Someone from out of town is here for the day.
Turn one stop into a day in town, and a day into a return trip.

---

## When something goes wrong

*A lost dog, a closed road, a power cut. A small town finds out fast, but not from one place.*

Help the town when something urgent happens, so the right people hear in time.

---

## Neighbours helping neighbours

*Most of a small town's economy never touches a cash register.*

Rides, a borrowed ladder, a shovelled driveway, a meal after a hard week.
Help the town trade favours, time and things, not only money.

---

## The owner with five minutes a week

*They open at seven, close at six and do the books at night.*

A local business owner has almost no time for the internet.
What would make the portal worth five minutes of their week?

---

## Word of mouth

*In a small town, the best recommendation has always come from a neighbour.*

Help residents vouch for the places and people they trust.

---

## Something to do this weekend

*"There's nothing to do here" usually means nobody told you.*

Show residents what's already happening in town, and send them to the people making it happen.

---

## A reason to stay

*Young people leave small towns for work. Some would stay if they could see a way.*

Help young people in town find work, experience and a future close to home.
