# Principles

These are the values your build is judged against.

They aren't features.
They're the lens for every decision you make, especially the ones where honoring them makes things harder.

The platforms that run small towns' online lives today are built to grow the platform.
These principles point the same kind of machine at the town instead: its residents, its businesses, its news and its groups.

---

## 1. Useful without an account

The site should do real good for someone who never logs in.
Signing up is the adoption we don't want to require.

---

## 2. Don't stand in the way

Send people to local businesses, news and groups. Don't put yourself between them.

---

## 3. Tell the truth about everyone

Every fact on the site should be traceable, correctable and removable.

---

## 4. Groups, not people

The portal learns from everyone who uses it.
Businesses see the town, not the people in it, and nobody's personal details reach a business unless that person chose it.

## When principles conflict

They will.

There's no fixed ranking.
Use your judgment, make the call, and tell us in `SUBMISSION.md` what you chose, what you gave up and why.
A clear, honest explanation of a hard call scores better than pretending there wasn't one.
